\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Implications for the prevalence literature}
\label{sec:discussion:prevalence}

\RESULT{State what the measured error rates imply for the published figures, in terms
of direction and magnitude. Be precise about which studies are affected and how: a
study that scores long pull request bodies is in a different position from one that
scores commit messages. Avoid the temptation to a single headline multiplier --- the
error is stratum-dependent, and that is itself the finding.}

\RESULT{Set the result against \citet{khosravani2026census}. They measure undercounting
in declaration channels; we measure overcounting in content-based detection. If both
hold, prevalence should be reported as an interval bounded from below by declaration and
from above by corrected detection, and neither bound alone is a defensible estimate.}

\subsection{Implications for platform-level attribution}
\label{sec:discussion:platform}

\RESULT{Argue from the \AidevTrailerRate{} trailer rate and the platform-account
channel: declared attribution works where it exists, and is cheap. The infrastructural
recommendation follows --- treat \texttt{Co-authored-by} and equivalent metadata as
first-class, and prefer declaration over inference wherever a choice exists.}

\subsection{Implications for research practice}
\label{sec:discussion:practice}

\RESULT{Concrete recommendations for future prevalence studies: validate in domain
before estimating; report per-genre and per-agent rather than pooled; exclude pre-LLM
automation explicitly and report it; state the operating point and its measured error;
and correct the estimate rather than reporting apparent prevalence. Frame as a
checklist, which is the form most likely to be adopted.}

\subsection{Ethical considerations}
\label{sec:discussion:ethics}

\RESULT{Detector output is already used in governance contexts --- contribution
policies, review triage, moderation. If false positive rates are elevated and unevenly
distributed across linguistic populations, deploying these tools as gatekeeping
mechanisms imposes costs on contributors who are already marginal. State this plainly
and connect to the measured stratified error rates rather than asserting it in the
abstract.}
